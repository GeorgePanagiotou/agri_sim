**Data findings to know**

- **NASA POWER rain disagrees with IMERG.** POWER gives about 1,000 mm for 2020 and for 2021; IMERG gives
  520–640 mm. POWER soil wetness follows POWER's own rain and has a model floor (GWETROOT ≈ 0.53). It rated
  Jan–Mar 2025, the driest season, as "wet". **Use IMERG for rain.** POWER wetness is context only.
- **ECOSTRESS JET ET is in mm/day.** The "W/m²" tag in the files is wrong (our earlier check). Stylianos's code
  keeps "W/m²". To fix.
- Sentinel-2 from processing baseline 04.00 (Jan 2022) carries a −1000 offset in its raw values. The new
  downloader removes it and records it in each file's JSON.
- **The Sentinel-2 vs HLS NDVI difference depends on greenness; it is not a constant offset.** With 24 same-day
  pairs over 6 seasons (Stylianos had 6):
  - Bare or dry dates: Sentinel-2 reads 0.03–0.11 lower.
  - Peak green (NDVI > 0.75): Sentinel-2 reads up to 0.06 higher.
  - Linear fit: **S2 = −0.124 + 1.200 × HLS** (r = 0.991). It halves the mean same-day difference, from 0.057
    to 0.028. EVI2: S2 = −0.070 + 1.226 × HLS.
  - A gain + offset correction fits much better than the single −0.061 shift. Not applied yet.
- **SMAP soil temperature (0–5 cm) in Nov–Dec 2024 was 10.7–15.8 °C.** The crop minimum soil temperatures
  (1.3–6.1 °C) don't bind, so moisture decides the sowing weeks.
- HLS PELT finds green-up in 3 of 6 seasons (2020–21, 2022–23, 2024–25), as in Stylianos's run.

## 2b. ML data for Stylianos (evening)

- **Training table:** `python -m agrisim.pipelines.build_training_table` builds
  `data/processed/ml/training_table_v1.parquet` (+ `.json`). See [docs/ml_training_data.md](docs/ml_training_data.md).
  - Rows: 117,272 rainfed field pixels × seasons, 2002–2025.
  - Targets: spring peak NDVI and crop vs fallow (satellite stand-ins).
  - 50 features: history, autumn NDVI, IMERG rain, risk, sowing window, soil, terrain, burn.
  - Decision date set by `--as-of`.
- **First XGBoost tests** (separate, not committed; notes in the scratchpad `ml_tests/ML_TEST_NOTES.md`):
  - **At 1 Dec, XGBoost does not beat the pixel's 5-year mean on new seasons.** Weather features alone have no
    skill: the weather is regional, and the outcome depends on rain after the decision date.
  - **At 1 Mar it does:** spring vigour R² 0.49, crop vs fallow AUC 0.80, both above the baselines, on unseen
    seasons and on 2018–2025. All of that skill comes from the Dec–Feb NDVI, i.e. in-season monitoring, not a
    forecast.
  - Spatial-block CV looks better but misleads, because the regional weather identifies the season. Use
    leave-one-season-out and forward splits.

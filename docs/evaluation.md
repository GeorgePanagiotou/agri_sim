# Evaluation plan

- **Phenology**: report usable dates per field-season, gap width between last clear low-greenness and first clear sustained positive, and missing transitions. Compare to independent dated field observations where possible.
- **Sowing success**: hold out entire farms, report sample counts, outcome prevalence, AUC with confidence intervals once n is sufficient, calibration and split provenance.
- **Days to emergence**: MAE in days, error stratified by crop, season, farm and cloud gap. Do not claim precise day from satellite alone.
- **Rotation simulation**: document Kc, capacity, soil depth, missing inputs, scenario assumptions; do sensitivity tests, never present predicted yield as a measured truth.
- **Operational limitations**: all current gridded climate sources are historical observations/reanalysis, not a future forecast.
